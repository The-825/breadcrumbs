Book and essays

The reasoning behind the kit.

The book gives the architecture and the decisions behind it. The papers below explain the memory method. Breadcrumbs supplies the practical files you can copy and adapt.

The book

From Archivist to Architect

A Practitioner's Field Guide to Building Better Information Systems, by Mr. Jovan Smith. A field guide for coordinators, advisors, analysts and program leaders who want to turn the spreadsheet everyone depends on into a system someone else can trust, run and improve.

Breadcrumbs is its free companion: checklists, templates and skills. You can use them without buying the book.
