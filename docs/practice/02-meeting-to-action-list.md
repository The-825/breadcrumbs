# Meeting to action list

**Status:** public, pattern-only exercise. No meeting transcription or task writer ships here.

**Assumes:** you have notes you may use and a place to save a reviewed action list. Use only the fictional notes below for this exercise. An assistant may organize text but cannot assign people or approve work.

Meeting notes often mix decisions, suggestions, and unanswered questions. If a suggestion turns into an assignment without review, someone can be held to work they never accepted.

## Fictional notes

> A: "We decided to use the smaller room for the workshop."
>
> B: "I can send a draft layout on Wednesday if the venue confirms the tables."
>
> C: "Who will bring sign-in sheets? We have not decided."
>
> A: "Keep the draft agenda open until the workshop lead replies."

## Your action

Make one action list with columns for type (decision, proposed action, unresolved question), exact supporting passage, owner, date, and confirmation status. Mark the room choice as a stated decision. Mark B's offer as conditional. Leave the sign-in-sheet owner `not decided`. Carry forward the waiting agenda item from your [briefing](01-inbox-to-briefing.md), if you completed it.

## Human check

Ask participants to confirm the room decision, B's condition, and any assignment or date before using the list to direct work. Record corrections in the list. A quote shows what the notes say; it does not prove everyone agreed. Save the reviewed list.
