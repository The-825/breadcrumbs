# Small-change request

> Assumes a team has a person or role that can approve a limited change. Adapt approval steps to your own policy. This is a pattern-only worksheet; it does not grant approval or trigger a deployment.

Use this request for a change that can be described, reviewed, and reversed without a large program plan. If the change affects safety, legal duties, sensitive information, critical service, or many teams, route it through the appropriate formal review process.
